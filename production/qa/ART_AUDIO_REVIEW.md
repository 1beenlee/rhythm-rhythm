## 1. Reference Study & Core Design Principles Applied

In accordance with `design/v0.2-art-audio-production-brief.md`, we analyzed the presentation grammar of *Rhythm Tengoku* (GBA), *Rhythm Heaven* (DS), *Rhythm Heaven Fever* (Wii), and *Rhythm Heaven Groove* (Switch):

1. **Full-Bleed Illustrated Stage Worlds (88–90% of Viewport)**:
   - Eliminated the v0.1 stack of four full-width bottom buttons (`TAP THE BEAT`, `PAUSE / RESUME`, `RESTART TRACK`, `EXIT TO GAMES`) and the generic bottom note-highway bar.
   - The cartoon stage itself is the interactive surface: clicking/tapping anywhere on the stage submits `"tap"` for 1-button games, and clicking/tapping the left or right half of the stage submits `"left"` or `"right"` for *Rhythm Kitchen*.
   - A sleek top bar holds only the episode title, `READY? / FOLLOW THE SOUND` status cue, countdown indicator, and a compact `II PAUSE` button that opens a centered **Show Paused** modal card.
2. **Bold 2D Cartoon Line Art & Character Acting (`core/cartoon_draw.gd`)**:
   - Consistent `3.5–4.5px` dark-ink outlines (`#1d1716`), expressive eyes (`normal`, `happy ^ ^`, `squint > <`, `wide O_O`, `dizzy X_X`), animated mouths (`smile`, `big_grin`, `open_o`, `open_a`, `frown`, `cat :3`), and the iconic Rhythm Heaven `"YOU"` badge anchored to the player's character.
   - Every stage features **4 distinct character acting states**:
     - **Idle Beat Bob**: Synchronized to `current_beat()` at the track's BPM (`104`, `96`, `102`).
     - **Pre-Hit Anticipation (`0.0..0.55` beats before event)**: Lever pullback & spring compression (*Beat Factory*), satellite dish tilt & wide-eyed inhale (*Echo Planet*), chef lean & burner flare (*Rhythm Kitchen*).
     - **Hit Impact (`Perfect` / `Good`)**: Squash-and-stretch recoil, comic starbursts, embossed golden crown/star parcel seals, neon concert stars, pan flip / bowl splash, and cheering background observers (Supervisor Bird / Sous-Chef Cat Nori).
     - **Comedic Miss (`Miss`)**: Squashed gray parcel with `X_X` eyes and puff of smoke (*Beat Factory*), dark dizzy planet & sweat drop (*Echo Planet*), counter ingredient splat & chef hat pop (*Rhythm Kitchen*).

## 3. Original Music & SFX Breakdown (`assets/audio/`)

All audio files are deterministically generated by `tools/generate_audio_assets.py` at **44,100 Hz, 16-bit PCM Stereo** and documented in `assets/audio/PROVENANCE.json`:

1. **`beat_factory_song.wav` (104 BPM, 42.0s, 7,408,844 bytes)**:
   - **Style**: Funky Industrial Pop / Clavinet Bounce (`C7 - F7 - G7 - C7`).
   - **Structure**: Bars 0–1 intro whistle & groove setup → Bars 2–7 main assembly verse → Bars 8–11 syncopated machine bridge → Bars 12–17 full brass/clavinet climax.
   - **Embedded Cues**: Crisp two-tap factory steam-whistle cue embedded on every `calls` beat (`880Hz → 1174.66Hz`) preceding the stamp target beat.
   - **World SFX**: `beat_factory_perfect.wav` (heavy pneumatic press thud + bright brass seal chime), `beat_factory_good.wav` (soft box thud), `beat_factory_miss.wav` (off-center clank + spring boing).
2. **`echo_planet_song.wav` (96 BPM, 36.0s, 6,350,444 bytes)**:
   - **Style**: Dreamy Space Synth-Pop / Cosmic Choir (`Fmaj7 - G6 - Em7 - Am7`).
   - **Structure**: Six 8-beat call-and-response cycles (`4 beats Alien Call` → `4 beats Player Echo`) with lush stereo pad chorus, space arpeggiator, and round sub-bass.
   - **Embedded Cues**: Formant-filtered alien lead synth (`Luma-Lou`) sings the exact 3-note rhythmic motif during the first 4 beats of each phrase (`E5`, `G5`, `A5`, `C6`).
   - **World SFX**: `echo_planet_perfect.wav` (crystalline harmonic fifth chime + Pip's answer voice), `echo_planet_good.wav` (soft synth pluck), `echo_planet_miss.wav` (detuned radio static fizzle).
3. **`rhythm_kitchen_song.wav` (102 BPM, 42.0s, 7,408,844 bytes)**:
   - **Style**: Upbeat Acoustic Bossa-Pop / Perky Marimba & Accordion (`G - Em7 - Am7 - D7`).
   - **Structure**: Sunny acoustic guitar/marimba intro → alternating single-toss verse → rapid left/right double-toss bridge → festive finale climax.
   - **Embedded Stereo Cues**: Prep toss cues panned **Left** (woodblock prep) before Left Skillet beats and **Right** (bright triangle prep) before Right Mixing Bowl beats.
   - **World SFX**: `rhythm_kitchen_perfect_left.wav` (stereo-left skillet sizzle + pan flip), `rhythm_kitchen_perfect_right.wav` (stereo-right ceramic bowl chime + splash), `rhythm_kitchen_good.wav`, `rhythm_kitchen_miss.wav` (comic splat).
4. **Result Fanfares**:
   - `fanfare_superb.wav` (upbeat C-major ninth brass/chime celebration) and `fanfare_ok.wav` (warm two-chord nod).
